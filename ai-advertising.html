The idea

More than a haircut.
A moment to reset.

The creative idea frames a haircut as a personal pause: a little time to feel composed, confident and ready for what comes next.

A restrained editorial portrait, soft daylight and oversized typography bring the idea to life. The visual focuses on one feeling rather than competing offers or discounts.

Independent portfolio campaign by Ansar. Not commissioned by, approved by or affiliated with Mori Cuts. The model and setting are AI-generated and fictional; the image does not depict a real customer or a haircut performed by this business. No campaign performance results are claimed.
